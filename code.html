UNSUPERVISED CLUSTERING ARCHITECTURE

8-Stage Analytical Machine Learning Pipeline

From heterogeneous synoptic readings to verified physical rainfall regimes. Each transformation maintains statistical integrity without target variable supervision.

01 cloud_download

Historical Ingestion

Streaming 145,460 rows of BOM weather logs spanning 10 years and 49 continental monitoring posts.

02 cleaning_services

Preprocessing & Screening

Robust IQR anomaly filtering, selective median and KNN-based imputation for missing atmospheric readings.

03 tune

Feature Normalization

StandardScaler transforms continuous variables (Pressure, Humidity, Temp) to zero mean and unit variance.

04 hub

Partition Clustering

Multi-restart K-Means alongside Medoids (PAM) with L1/L2 distance metric optimization across candidate k.

05 account_tree

Hierarchical Linkage

Ward minimum variance hierarchical agglomeration verifying dendritic structure and regime bifurcation.

06 fact_check

Validation Indices

Silhouette Coefficient and Davies-Bouldin Index (DBI) evaluation to identify k=4 as optimal mathematical peak.

07 view_in_ar

PCA Coordinate Projection

Eigenvector dimensionality reduction to Principal Components (PC1, PC2, PC3) capturing 78.4% variance.

08 insights

Climatological Profiling

Meteorological translation: mapping unsupervised mathematical centroids back to physical rain regime identities.
